About

Mwanzo Community Library opened in 2016 with 400 donated books and two tables. Today we hold over 9,000 titles, run a free after-school reading club, and lend books to more than 1,200 registered members.

We are funded entirely by membership fees and donations. Everything we own was given to us, and everything we lend comes back — mostly.

What We Offer

Borrowing

Up to four titles at a time, for three weeks, renewable once. Fiction, non-fiction, reference and a growing children's section.

Study Space

Twenty-four quiet desks, power sockets at every table, and free Wi-Fi. First come, first served.

Reading Club

Free after-school sessions for ages 6 to 14, every Tuesday and Thursday. No registration needed — just turn up.
